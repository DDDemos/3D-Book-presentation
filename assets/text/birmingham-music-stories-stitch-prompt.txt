Birmingham Music Stories

Design a visually striking, interactive cultural heritage website called **Birmingham Music Stories**.

The website should celebrate Birmingham's musical heritage by connecting **music, people and places across the city**.

This is not a commercial music-streaming website and should not look like Spotify or a generic corporate landing page. It should feel like a contemporary digital museum exhibition: editorial, atmospheric, artistic and distinctly connected to Birmingham.

## Visual direction

Combine the feeling of:

* archival music photography
* old gig posters and record sleeves
* Birmingham's industrial heritage
* contemporary museum/exhibition design
* subtle analogue textures and print imperfections

Use a sophisticated dark visual style with:

* deep charcoal / near-black backgrounds
* warm off-white typography
* muted brass or amber accents
* occasional deep red accents
* large expressive editorial typography
* subtle grain and texture
* strong use of spacing and oversized imagery

Avoid a generic AI-generated SaaS aesthetic, excessive rounded cards, glassmorphism and bright neon gradients.

The design should feel credible enough to belong to a Birmingham cultural organisation or music heritage exhibition.

## Homepage structure

### 1. Hero

Large title:

**BIRMINGHAM
MUSIC STORIES**

Subtitle:

**The sounds, people and places that helped shape a city.**

Include a strong visual composition inspired by music posters, photography and Birmingham's industrial architecture.

Primary call to action:

**Explore the stories ↓**

Include a small line such as:

**Heavy Metal • Reggae • New Wave • Bhangra • Rock • Soul**

### 2. Introduction

Create a short editorial introduction explaining that Birmingham's musical identity has been shaped by different communities, neighbourhoods, venues and generations.

Use large typography rather than a standard text box.

### 3. Explore Birmingham's Sounds

Create a visually interesting collection of categories:

**Heavy Metal**
**Reggae**
**New Wave**
**Bhangra**
**Rock & Pop**

Each category should feel like an exhibition poster rather than a normal UI card.

Hovering over a category should feel interactive.

### 4. Birmingham Sound Map

Create a stylised illustrated map of Birmingham rather than a conventional Google Map.

Place several interactive-looking locations around the map, including:

* Aston
* Handsworth
* Birmingham City Centre
* Broad Street

The design should suggest that clicking a location will reveal the music stories connected with that part of Birmingham.

Title:

**A City of Sound**

Subtitle:

**Explore the places behind the music.**

### 5. Featured Stories

Create editorial story panels for:

**Black Sabbath — Aston**
*The sound that helped define heavy metal.*

**Steel Pulse — Handsworth**
*Reggae, identity and Birmingham's multicultural sound.*

**UB40 — Birmingham**
*Reggae from the city to audiences around the world.*

**Duran Duran — The Rum Runner**
*New Romantic music emerging from Birmingham nightlife.*

**Electric Light Orchestra — Birmingham**
*Rock, pop and orchestration on an international scale.*

Each story should prominently display the artist, place and musical movement.

### 6. Music Through Time

Design a horizontal visual timeline moving from the late 1960s through the 1970s, 1980s and into contemporary Birmingham music.

Keep it highly visual rather than text heavy.

### 7. Final section

Large statement:

**Every city has a soundtrack.
This one changed music.**

Button:

**Discover Birmingham**

Footer:

**Birmingham Music Stories — A digital exploration of the city's musical heritage.**

## Interaction design

Design clear states for:

* hovering over story cards
* selecting music genres
* selecting locations on the Birmingham map
* opening a featured story
* scrolling through the timeline

The final result should feel immersive and exploratory while remaining realistic enough to implement as a simple responsive website.

Design both a polished **desktop view** and a clear **mobile-responsive version**.
